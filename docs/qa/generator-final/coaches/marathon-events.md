## Sources and limitations

Public primary sources were retrieved in the locked initial review and remain the coaching references: https://www.baa.org/races/boston-marathon/info-for-athletes/boston-marathon-training/ and https://www.halhigdon.com/training-programs/marathon-training/novice-1-marathon/ illustrate sustained marathon endurance and recovery, including valid four-day approaches. https://www.halhigdon.com/training-programs/half-marathon-training/intermediate-2-half-marathon/ supports conversational easy work and event-specific development. They do not create universal distance/week minima. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17762369/ provides a taper framework, not clearance after interruption. I made no proprietary Runna implementation assumptions.

No cause of break, fatigue, pain, injury history or actual outcome is established. Logged synthetic seconds exist in U05, U06, U09 and U10; U01, U02, U03, U04, U07 and U08 primary completed logs have no sec. Detailed counts and actual examples are in JSON. No missing seconds were invented. Recorded historical session pace, measured prediction and current prescription are different evidence categories. Explicit skip does not justify asserting actual work performed on the skipped day. Full/limited labels and 10/12-week or mileage cutoffs are internal rules; readiness ultimately requires current repeatable tolerance and event durability. Input-purity flags are provided by the serialized domain probes; this review does not independently prove live object identity, UI gating or persistence.
